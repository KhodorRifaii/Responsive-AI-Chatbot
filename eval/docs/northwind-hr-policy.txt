Northwind Logistics: Employee Policy Handbook (version 4.2)

1. Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to 9 public holidays. Leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 5 working days before it starts. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year, and they expire on 31 March.

2. Sick leave
Employees are paid in full for their first 10 sick days each year. From the 11th day, sick pay is 50% of the normal salary, for up to 60 days in total. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive sick day. Employees must tell their manager before 09:00 on the first day of absence.

3. Parental leave
The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks. Parental leave can be taken within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption, in one block or in two blocks.

4. Probation and notice
New employees start with a probation period of 90 days. During probation, either side can end the contract with 14 days of notice. After probation, the notice period is 60 days for both the employee and the company.

5. Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. On every working day, everyone must be reachable during the core hours of 10:00 to 15:00. A manager can approve more remote days for a limited time. Working from another country is allowed for at most 20 days per calendar year and needs written approval from HR for tax reasons.

6. Expenses
Business expenses must be submitted in the Expense Portal within 30 days of the purchase, with the receipt attached. When travelling, meals are reimbursed up to 40 US dollars per day. Taxi rides need a receipt when they cost more than 15 US dollars. Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy class. Expenses submitted after the 30-day window are not reimbursed.

7. Equipment
Every employee gets a company laptop, which is replaced every 36 months. New remote workers receive a one-time home office stipend of 300 US dollars for a chair, desk or monitor. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to the IT desk on the same day.

8. Learning and development
Each employee has a training budget of 1,200 US dollars per year for courses, books and conferences. The training must be approved by the manager in advance. If an employee leaves within 12 months after a paid course, they repay the course fee.

9. Conduct and complaints
Northwind has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment and discrimination. Complaints can be sent to the confidential HR mailbox, and every complaint receives a first answer within 5 working days. Retaliation against someone who raised a concern in good faith is a disciplinary offence.
