Orbit R5 Wi-Fi 5 Router: Quick Guide

1. About the Orbit R5
The Orbit R5 is the entry model of the Orbit family. It is a dual-band Wi-Fi 5 router (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz) with a combined maximum speed of 1200 Mbps. It has 1 WAN port and 3 LAN ports, all running at 100 Mbps. It supports WPA2 security. The power adapter is 12 V and 1 A.

2. First setup
Connect the WAN port to the modem and plug in the power adapter. When the light turns solid green, the router is ready. Open a browser and go to 192.168.1.1. The admin password is printed on the label under the router and should be changed after the first login.

3. What the light means
Solid green: the router is online.
Blinking green: the router is starting up.
Blinking amber: the router is too hot. Move it to a ventilated place and make sure nothing covers the vents.
Solid red: no connection to the modem. Check the WAN cable.

4. Factory reset
Hold the reset button for 8 seconds until the light blinks green. The Wi-Fi name and password return to the values on the label.

5. Updates and guest network
The router installs new firmware automatically at 02:00. The guest network supports up to 4 devices and stays on until you switch it off. There are no parental controls on this model.

6. Warranty
The Orbit R5 has a warranty of 12 months from the purchase date. Keep the receipt. The warranty does not cover damage caused by overheating in a closed cabinet.
